Essora Mail Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Overview

Essora Mail is a desktop email client developed for Essora Linux. The application allows users to access, read, send and manage email from supported email providers.

Information accessed

When a user connects an email account, Essora Mail may access information required to provide email functionality, including:

  • Email address and account identity.
  • Email messages and message metadata.
  • Email folders and labels.
  • Attachments.
  • Authentication tokens required to maintain the account connection.

How information is used

Email account data is used only to provide features requested by the user, including reading, composing, sending, replying to, organizing and searching email.

Essora does not use email content for advertising, profiling or unrelated analytics.

Google account access

When Google authentication is used, Essora Mail uses Google OAuth 2.0. The user's Google password is never collected or stored by Essora Mail.

Authentication tokens may be stored locally on the user's computer so the user does not need to sign in every time the application starts.

Local processing

Essora Mail is designed as a local desktop application. Email data is exchanged directly between the user's computer and the configured email provider.

Essora Mail does not require email messages to pass through an Essora-operated mail server.

Data sharing

Essora Mail does not sell personal information and does not sell, rent or share email content with advertisers or data brokers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Essora Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention

Account settings, locally cached mail data and authentication tokens may remain on the user's computer until the account is removed, the user signs out, or the application data is deleted.

Revoking Google access

Users can disconnect their Google account from Essora Mail and may also revoke Essora Mail access from their Google Account security settings.

Security

Essora Mail uses encrypted connections when communicating with supported mail providers and aims to store authentication information locally with restricted filesystem permissions.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Essora Mail evolves. The current version will always be published on this page.
