Essora Mail

Essora Mail is a lightweight graphical desktop email client designed for simplicity. It aims to make reading, sending and managing email easy for everyday users without exposing complex mail configuration.

Simple by design

  • Easy account setup.
  • Read and send email.
  • Multiple accounts and folders.
  • Attachments, search and message organization.
  • Google OAuth authentication for Gmail.
  • Native C and GTK3 interface.

Privacy

Essora Mail is designed as a local desktop application. Email content is accessed directly from the user's email provider and is not routed through Essora servers.
